The most interesting things about us are often left unwritten.

The thoughts we keep to ourselves. The memories we almost forget. The opinions we've never had to explain. The questions we've never thought to ask.

There's an entire world beneath the surface of our everyday lives.

That's what The Underground is about. Not telling you what to think, but giving you a reason to discover what you think.

Some questions deserve more than a passing thought.

THE ONE QUESTION A DAY METHOD

Scan. Think. Write.

A simple daily ritual that brings together digital discovery and the personal experience of writing by hand.

01 / SCAN

Start with a scan.

Open your physical journal and scan the QR code inside. The planned digital companion will reveal a new question each day, giving you somewhere to begin.

02 / THINK

Follow your curiosity.

Some questions will challenge your beliefs. Others will bring back memories, present moral dilemmas or make you see something familiar in a new way.

03 / WRITE

Make the answer yours.

Close your phone and put pen to paper. There are no correct answers, no word limits and no expectations. Just your thoughts, in your own words.

The phone gives you the question. The journal holds your answer.

THE PHYSICAL JOURNAL

More than your answers. A reflection of your life.

Your answers are only one part of your story.

The journal also gives you space to remember everyday moments, track your habits, set monthly goals and reflect on the things that matter to you.

Choose your cover, your paper, your duration and the extra pages you want to include.

Over time, what begins as a blank journal becomes something that could only belong to you.
